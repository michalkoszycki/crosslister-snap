Snap

a crosslisting app

What Snap is

Snap lists what you photograph. Take the photos of an item on your phone; Snap writes the listing, with a title, a price and a description, and posts it on eBay and Craigslist, on your own accounts.

Snap is run by its developer; contact: the Feedback box in Admin, or the email below.

crosslistersnap@gmail.com

Privacy

What the server keeps, and why:

  • Your photos, and the listings made from them, so they can be posted, changed and ended.
  • Your eBay token, so Snap can list for you on your eBay account.
  • Your email address, to send you sign-in links.
  • Your seller address, for shipping and pickup on your listings.
  • Usage counts: how many postings you made and how many you have left.

To write a listing, the photos you mark and your note are sent to the AI service that drafts it.

What it never keeps: your card details. Payments go to Stripe, and card numbers are typed only on Stripe's own page.

eBay can ask us to delete your eBay data (for example when you close your eBay account), and we do.

Deleting your account is, for now, a request to the developer: write in the Feedback box or to the email above.

The app loads nothing from other sites: no ads, no analytics, no trackers.

Terms

  • Snap is a tool that lists what you photograph on your own accounts. You are the seller: what you list, its price and its description, and dealing with your buyers are yours, under eBay's and Craigslist's own rules.
  • Your first three postings are free; after that, postings are prepaid. Postings already used are not refunded.
  • The service may stop or change at any time.
